About

“The singer with SPB's voice.”Cineulagam Podcast · translated from Tamil

Ramu Maharajapuram is a playback and concert singer whose recordings span Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Sanskrit. His singing moves easily between film songs, the light-music repertoire made beloved by S. P. Balasubrahmanyam, and devotional music.

He has sung for films including Partner and Kasthuri Veedu, and has released a steady body of albums and singles on Apple Music, JioSaavn and other platforms. He travels to perform for audiences in India and abroad.

Around the world

Bringing Ramu to your city

Ramu travels to perform for audiences abroad. For organisers planning an overseas concert, sharing these details early helps his team plan the visit well.

  • Dates and city. Some flexibility on dates helps fit a visit into a wider tour.
  • Travel and stay. Whether flights, visa support and accommodation can be arranged.
  • Musicians. A full orchestra, a small ensemble, local musicians or tracks.
  • Other cities. Organisers in nearby cities can be included in one tour.
