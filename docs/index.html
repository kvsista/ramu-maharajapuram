About

A voice audiences recognise

Ramu Maharajapuram grew up in Nellore, S. P. Balasubrahmanyam's home town, learning SPB's songs from cassettes. Audiences and the media know him as “SPB Ramu” because his voice so closely resembles SPB's.

His breakthrough came on SPB's ETV show Paadutha Theeyaga in 1996. Around 2000 he moved to Chennai, where he became a sought-after track singer for composers such as Bharadwaj, G. Anand and Vasu Rao, and by his own count sang 200–300 guide tracks for SPB himself. He twice won the Andhra Pradesh State Nandi TV Award for Best Male Playback Singer: for the serial Mano Yagnam (2001) and the title song of Thrisoolam (2007). He has recorded more than 100 albums and singles since 2000, sung for the films Partner and Kasthuri Veedu, and performed more than 2,000 concerts in India, the USA and Canada.

Short biography

Ramu Maharajapuram is a playback and concert singer from Nellore, Andhra Pradesh, based in Chennai. Known to audiences as “SPB Ramu” for a voice that closely recalls S. P. Balasubrahmanyam's, he came to notice on SPB's ETV show Paadutha Theeyaga in 1996 and went on to sing 200 to 300 guide tracks for SPB. He has twice won the Andhra Pradesh Nandi TV Award for Best Male Playback Singer, recorded more than 100 albums and singles in Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Sanskrit, and performed more than 2,000 concerts in India, the USA and Canada.
