About

A voice audiences recognise

Ramu Maharajapuram is a playback and concert singer. Audiences call him “Andhra Balu” because his voice so closely resembles that of S. P. Balasubrahmanyam.

He has given more than 2,500 stage performances in India, the USA, Canada, the Middle East and South Africa. He has sung more than 250 songs for Telugu and Tamil films and released more than 750 audio albums in Telugu, Tamil and Kannada. His devotional bhajans are heard in temples across Andhra Pradesh. He has twice received the Andhra Pradesh State Nandi Award for best singer in television, in 2002 and 2008.
