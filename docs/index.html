Playback & Concert Singer · Voice Artist

Ramu Maharajapuram, known as “SPB Ramu”, is a two-time Nandi Award-winning playback singer whose voice recalls the great S. P. Balasubrahmanyam. He has recorded more than 750 albums, sung over 250 film songs, including in Attarintiki Daredi, and given more than 2,500 stage performances in India and abroad.

About

A voice audiences recognise

Ramu Maharajapuram grew up in Nellore, S. P. Balasubrahmanyam's home town, and learned to sing from SPB's cassettes. Audiences and the media know him as “SPB Ramu” because his voice so closely resembles SPB's.

Since his breakthrough on SPB's own show Paadutha Theeyaga in 1996 he has sung 200–300 guide tracks for SPB himself, won two Nandi TV Awards, recorded more than 750 albums and sung over 250 film songs. Today he sings for audiences in India, the USA, Canada, the Middle East and South Africa, and works as a playback singer and voice artist.

His work

What Ramu does

Film playback

Playback singing for film composers and producers in Telugu, Tamil and Kannada.

Concerts & SPB evenings

Film-music and light-music concerts, including evenings celebrating the songs of S. P. Balasubrahmanyam.

International concerts

Performances for cultural associations, festivals and charities outside India.

Temple festivals & bhakti

Devotional concerts with bhajans, keerthanas and songs suited to the deity and occasion.

Celebrations

Weddings, receptions and milestone events, with a programme shaped around the occasion.

Albums & recordings

Studio work for producers and labels in any of his four languages.

Voice & dubbing

Character voices for films and television, as for Basava in Sing Geetham, plus voice-overs and narration.

Television

Music shows and tributes, as on ETV’s Swarabhishekam and Paadutha Theeyaga.
