Biography

Ramu Maharajapuram is a playback and concert singer from Nellore, Andhra Pradesh, based in Chennai. Known to audiences as “SPB Ramu” for a voice that closely recalls S. P. Balasubrahmanyam's, he came to notice on SPB's own ETV show Paadutha Theeyaga in 1996 and went on to record 200 to 300 guide tracks for SPB himself. He has twice won the Andhra Pradesh Nandi TV Award for Best Male Playback Singer and has sung for films including Attarintiki Daredi, Partner and Kasthuri Veedu. With 280 albums and singles in Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Sanskrit since 1998, and more than 2,000 concerts in India, the USA and Canada, he carries SPB's songs to audiences around the world.
